Theme mockups

Four looks for the same page. The HTML is identical in all four; only the stylesheet changes.

  • Cream (css): the current look rebuilt from scratch, with the live site's title layout and a sticky sidebar on wide screens.
  • Book (css): one quiet serif column, contents in the margin, and the site map as an index at the end.
  • Arcade (css): loud and playful, with the article on a card and the site map as a grid of section cards.
  • Plain (css): about 30 lines of CSS, with browser defaults for everything else.
  • UMN (css): Minnesota maroon and gold, with a maroon band on top and a sidebar.
  • USD (css): South Dakota red and black, with the site map as a bar across the top.
  • Notebook (css): graph paper, handwritten headings, taped-in pictures, and a sticky note for contents.
  • Midnight (css): dark first, with numbered sections and the contents in the left margin.
  • KU (css): Kansas blue and crimson, with a full-width title banner and the site map on the right.
  • Tailwind palette (css): pale but colorful shades from Tailwind's named colors, with six hues taking turns.
  • Swiss (css): big grotesque type and heavy rules, with headings hanging in a left column.
  • Terminal (css): monospace at 80 columns, with the contents and site map drawn as directory trees.
  • Cards (css): a playing card laid face up on a red card back, with the site map dealt as a spread of cards.
  • Deck (css): the Cards background with KU's title band; the contents and asides are small cards hanging over the article's edge and the band.
  • UMN with asides (css): UMN with a margin on the right where the contents and asides float, as on the live site.

Deck and UMN with asides add one demo aside to the shared HTML, so the floating asides have something to show. Its text is a note that is commented out at that spot in isomorphic.md.
